Shopping List is the smartest way to manage your everyday shopping.

Use it as a personal shopping memo, or share lists with your family and roommates to never forget an item again.

KEY FEATURES

Simple List Management
- Create shopping lists and add items in seconds
- Check off purchased items with a single tap
- Drag and drop to reorder items freely

Group Sharing
- Share lists with family members and roommates
- Invite others with a simple URL link
- Real-time sync keeps everyone updated

Stay Organized
- Manage lists by store (supermarket, drugstore, etc.)
- Auto-sort items by category (produce, meat, household, etc.)
- Customize category order to match your store layout

Handy Extras
- Add notes to items (e.g., "the low-fat one")
- Color-code items for visual organization
- Duplicate past lists for easy reuse
- Auto-archive completed lists

PERFECT FOR
- People who frequently forget items
- Families sharing household shopping duties
- Anyone juggling multiple stores
- Those tired of texting "do we need anything?"

Whether shopping solo or as a group, Shopping List has you covered.
